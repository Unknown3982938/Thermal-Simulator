01 — PURPOSE

Educational Purpose

The Thermodynamics Laboratory is an interactive educational project intended to demonstrate selected concepts in thermodynamics through visual simulations and user-controlled experiments.

It aims to help learners develop a better understanding of relationships between physical quantities such as temperature, pressure, volume, and energy.

02 — SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS

Simplified Physical Models

The simulation uses simplified mathematical models and visual representations of physical phenomena. Actual thermodynamic systems may behave differently depending on their materials, environmental conditions, and other physical factors.

Numerical Accuracy

Displayed values and calculated results are intended to illustrate scientific relationships. They may involve approximations, assumptions, or simplified calculations.

Users should consult established scientific references and perform appropriate calculations when numerical precision is required.

Visual Representation

Particles, containers, pistons, heating elements, and other visual objects are graphical representations. Their appearance, movement, scale, and animation speed do not necessarily correspond to real-world dimensions or physical behavior.
