WHAT'S HAPPENING?

Sensible heat

When heat enters a substance without changing its phase, its internal energy increases. In this simplified model, the average particle kinetic energy rises, so temperature increases.

Q = mcΔT
Q = heat · m = mass · c = specific heat capacity · ΔT = temperature change
